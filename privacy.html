Privacy Policy

Effective from November 2026 · Aalynex Creator Space

Aalynex connects creators and editors to collaborate on projects and offers AI content tools. This policy explains how personal information may be processed when you use our website or Android app.

Information processed

Depending on features you use, Aalynex processes your email, profile name, optional phone, role, skills and portfolio links; project briefs and uploaded assets; messages and support requests; device push tokens; and account/security activity. AI tools process prompts and generated responses. Transactions may include payment status, order references and reconciliation data; payment card details are handled by the payment provider rather than stored as card numbers by the app.

Why the information is used

We use it to manage accounts, enable creator–editor collaboration, secure access to projects and downloads, deliver notifications, handle payments and disputes, operate AI tools, investigate reports, and comply with legal obligations.

Service providers

Our platform uses infrastructure and integrations including Supabase (authentication/database), Expo/Firebase (Android push), hosting/storage services, AI providers and Razorpay for relevant payment workflows. Providers may process only the information required for their services, subject to their policies.

Sharing and visibility

Public creator/editor profiles may be visible to other members. Project participants can see permitted collaboration data. Private chat content and protected files should only be accessible to authorized participants. Moderation and support personnel may review reported content. No guarantee of absolute internet security can be made.

Storage and deletion

You can request account and associated data deletion from within the app or from the public account deletion page. We will verify identity and review unresolved projects, payments, fraud/security matters and legal record-retention duties. Data not required to be retained will be deleted as part of processing the request. Any data lawfully retained is limited to the relevant purpose and retention obligations. For status or privacy questions contact support@aalynex.com.

Security and choices

We use authenticated access controls and row-level security for applicable datasets. You can manage notifications in Android settings and Aalynex. Do not share passwords or authentication codes. You can report abusive users/content through supported in-app reporting controls.

Children and restricted content

Users must comply with Aalynex's applicable age eligibility and community rules. We prohibit illegal, exploitative or harmful content. Parents or guardians may contact support with privacy concerns.

Policy updates

We may update this document as our products and legal requirements evolve. Changes will be published here.

Community Guidelines

Effective from November 2026 · Aalynex Creator Space

Aalynex is a professional collaboration space. These rules apply to chat, profiles, projects, uploaded media and AI-generated content.

Respect and safety

No harassment, threats, hateful conduct, bullying, exploitation, non-consensual personal content, impersonation or exposure of private information. Do not send unwanted repeated messages after a user asks you to stop.

Legitimate projects and payments

Post accurate project descriptions, own or have permission to use uploaded assets, and avoid fraud, deceptive listings or requests for secret credentials. Do not attempt to evade payment and download protections or misrepresent payment verification.

AI and copyright

Use AI tools responsibly, review generated output before publishing, and report harmful or offensive results. Do not use AI tools to facilitate illegal or exploitative activity. Respect intellectual property rights.

Reporting and blocking

Use Report User, Report Conversation or Report AI Output in the app. Blocking stops direct messaging between the two accounts. Reports are reviewed by moderators; false or malicious reporting may result in restrictions.

Moderation

Aalynex may investigate reports, restrict access to violating content and accounts, and cooperate with lawful requests, consistent with relevant laws and our privacy practices. Contact support@aalynex.com for safety matters.
